THE RETREAT PAGE — ADD ECO SECTION
New section titled How This Place Works:
Rocky Hills operates on a standalone solar and battery system. Water is harvested rainwater. Waste is managed on-site. We have made these choices deliberately — not as a selling point, but as a responsibility to the country this retreat sits on.
We hold Sustainable Tourism Certification with Ecotourism Australia. We are working toward carbon neutrality. These are ongoing commitments, not completed ones.
When you stay here, you are part of a system that tries to take less than it gives.

LOCATION PAGE
Rocky Hills sits on the traditional country of the Big River Nation and Oyster Bay Nation, on Tasmania's East Coast between Swansea and the Freycinet Peninsula. We acknowledge this country and the custodianship of those who have known it longest.
The nearest town is Swansea. The nearest airport is Hobart, two hours south.
There is one road in. That is the point.
